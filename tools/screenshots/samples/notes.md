## Formulas

The partial sums of 1 + 2 + 3 + … are $S_n = \sum_{i=1}^{n} i = \frac{n(n+1)}{2}$, which grow without bound.

$$\sum_{k=0}^{\infty} r^k = \frac{1}{1 - r}$$
