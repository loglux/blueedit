# Formula pictures

The definite integral worked out:

$$
\int_1^3 \left(3x^{5/2} + x^2\right)\,dx = \left[\frac{6}{7}x^{7/2} + \frac{x^3}{3}\right]_1^3
$$

The roots of a quadratic:

$$x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}$$

A system and a piecewise function:

\[
\begin{aligned} y &= 2x + 3 \\ y - 3 &= 2x \end{aligned}
\]

$$
f(x) = \begin{cases} x^2 & x \ge 0 \\ -x & x < 0 \end{cases}
$$

A sum: $$\sum_{k=1}^{n} k = \frac{n(n+1)}{2}$$

Text after the formulas, with an inline one: $\alpha \le \beta$.
